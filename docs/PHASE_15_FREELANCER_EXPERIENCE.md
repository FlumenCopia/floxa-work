# FLOXA WORK — PHASE 15 FREELANCER EXPERIENCE SPECIFICATION

**Target Audience:** Independent Consultants, Agency Leads, and Freelancers  
**Objective:** Rewarding Strategic Submissions, Transparent Contract Boundaries, and Frictionless Direct Settlement  

---

## 1. Journey Architecture

Freelancers in FLOXA Work do not compete in a "race-to-the-bottom" price war or generic bidding queue. The platform guides candidates through strategic discovery:

```
[1. Profile & Portfolio] ──► [2. Discovery & Invitations] ──► [3. Mini-Discovery Proposal]
                                                                      │
[5. Direct Milestone Settlement] ◄── [4. Project Execution] ◄─────────┘
```

---

## 2. Key Polish Enhancements

### 2.1 Mini-Discovery Proposal Creation
- **Diagnostic-First Pitch:**
  - Instead of generic cover letters, freelancers submit structured diagnostic answers demonstrating problem comprehension, root cause analysis, and proposed solutions.
- **Strategic Sliders:**
  - Freelancers set visual execution alignment sliders (e.g. speed vs. craft, consumer-friendly vs. enterprise) to calibrate client expectations prior to project launch.
- **Milestone Breakdown Definition:**
  - Every proposal breaks the project into distinct deliverables with target timelines (days) and budget allocations.
- **Evidence-Based Portfolio Links:**
  - Freelancers attach concrete links to live code, design artifacts, or case studies verifying their credentials.

### 2.2 Award & Workspace Transition
- Once accepted by the client:
  - An immutable contract snapshot is generated in the database.
  - The collaboration workspace is unlocked immediately for milestone tracking, task boards, discussions, and deliverable versioning.
  - The advance payment arrangement is displayed with bank/UPI/invoice settlement instructions.

### 2.3 Milestone Execution & Deliverable Submission
- **Versioned Deliverables:**
  - Freelancers upload milestone assets with clear file descriptions and external repository/preview links.
  - Built-in revision round tracking prevents scope creep while honoring client feedback.
- **Direct Settlement Invoicing:**
  - Upon deliverable signoff by the client, freelancers record their payment settlement reference (UTR, wire reference, or receipt).
  - Both parties maintain a clear, non-custodial audit trail without platform middlemen or escrow fees.
