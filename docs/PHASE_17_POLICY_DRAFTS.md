> [!CAUTION]

> The following documents are working drafts intended to guide beta platform operations. They do not constitute formal legal advice, certification, or binding legal representations. Prior to public launch, all documents must be formally reviewed and approved by qualified legal counsel in your operating jurisdiction.

---

## 1. Non-Custodial External Payment Disclosure (Permanent Policy)

**Effective Date:** Beta Launch 2026  
**Status:** Mandatory Platform Invariant  

1. **No Custody of Funds:** FLOXA Work is a discovery-first freelance marketplace and project collaboration platform. FLOXA Work **does not** hold client or freelancer funds, operate escrow accounts, maintain digital wallets or balances, process payment disbursements, or initiate financial refunds.
2. **Direct Bilateral Settlement:** All project payments—including initial milestone advances and subsequent milestone disbursements—are settled directly between the client and the freelancer using their mutually agreed external payment method (such as direct bank wire/NEFT/RTGS, UPI, Stripe, Razorpay direct invoices, or international remittances).
3. **User-Reported Records:** Payment entries, references (such as UTR numbers), and receipt attachments displayed within the platform represent records submitted directly by the respective client and freelancer. FLOXA Work coordinates milestones and proof-of-work documentation, but does not verify bank account transactions or act as a financial intermediary.
4. **No Financial Recovery Guarantee:** FLOXA Work does not guarantee the recovery of funds settled externally between parties. In the event of an external transaction error or fraudulent transfer, users must contact their banking institution or payment provider directly.

## 2. Terms of Service (Draft)

1. **Eligibility & Account Integrity:** Users must provide accurate, verifiable identity and portfolio credentials. Creating duplicate, fraudulent, or impersonated accounts is strictly prohibited.
2. **Contractual Agreement Snapshot:** When a client accepts a freelancer's Mini-Discovery proposal, FLOXA Work generates an immutable digital project record reflecting the agreed deliverables, duration, and milestone budget schedule. This snapshot governs the collaboration workspace.
3. **Decoupled Milestone Review:** Deliverable approval within the collaboration workspace signifies satisfaction with the submitted work artifact. Deliverable signoff does not automatically trigger financial debits from client accounts.
4. **Independent Contractor Relationship:** Freelancers are independent service providers, not employees, partners, or agents of FLOXA Work.

---

## 3. Privacy Policy (Draft)

1. **Data Minimization:** FLOXA Work collects only information necessary to operate the marketplace: name, professional handle, email address, portfolio links, skills, and project communication history.
2. **Zero Commercial Sale of User Data:** FLOXA Work does not sell, rent, or monetize personal information or project files to third parties or advertising brokers.
3. **Private File Vault Isolation:** Milestone deliverables and proprietary client assets are encrypted in storage and accessible only by verified contract participants and platform administrators resolving active disputes.
4. **Data Deletion Rights:** Users may request the permanent deletion of their account credentials and unassociated profile information upon request, subject to statutory fraud prevention requirements.

---

## 4. Dispute and Mediation Policy (Draft)

1. **Nature of Mediation:** FLOXA Work provides administrative and communication facilitation for project disagreements (such as deliverable quality, delayed milestones, or unacknowledged external payment records).
2. **Platform Mediation vs. Financial Arbitration:** Platform mediation is administrative and communicative, not legally binding financial arbitration. FLOXA Work operators review workspace activity, communication threads, and submitted UTR references to facilitate amicable resolution or take moderation action (e.g. account suspension), but cannot reverse bank transfers.
3. **Non-Custodial Dispute Boundary:** Because FLOXA Work does not hold escrow balances, dispute resolution does not include financial chargebacks or platform-funded payouts.

---

## 5. File Upload and Content Policy (Draft)

1. **Prohibited Content:** Uploading malicious code, viruses, malware, unlawful materials, stolen intellectual property, or offensive media is strictly prohibited and results in immediate permanent account termination.
2. **File Size & Type Limits:** File attachments are restricted to approved formats (`.zip`, `.pdf`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.json`, `.svg`, `.fig`) with an enforced 50MB per-file boundary.
3. **Intellectual Property Rights:** Intellectual property rights for deliverables transfer directly between freelancer and client in accordance with their contractual agreement upon external payment settlement.

---

## 6. Community Guidelines & Acceptable Use Policy (Draft)

1. **Professional Conduct:** Users must maintain professional, respectful communication in project discussions and chat channels. Harassment, discrimination, and abusive behavior will not be tolerated.
2. **Anti-Circumvention Integrity:** Utilizing the platform to solicit off-platform discovery spam or abusing platform features to harvest freelancer identities is strictly forbidden.
3. **Truth in Diagnosis:** Freelancers must provide genuine, strategic problem diagnoses in Mini-Discovery pitches and must not use automated scraping or deceptive portfolio representations.
